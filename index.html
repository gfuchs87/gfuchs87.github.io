Religion ✦ Media ✦ Politics

Gustavo Fuchs is a Ph.D. candidate at the Lozano Long Institute for Latin American Studies at the University of Texas at Austin. His research examines evangelical media and politics in Central America, human rights, and media concentration in Latin America. He holds an LL.M. in International Law from the University of Nottingham and an M.A. in Communication and Public Opinion from FLACSO.

Research

Dissertation

Creating the World Anew: Evangelical Media and Politics in Costa Rica and Guatemala. Expected defense: March 2027. Advisor: Dr. Brent Crosson.

Research interests

Religion and media in Latin America; evangelical politics and public discourse; spiritual warfare and digital pilgrimage; media concentration and press freedom; comparative Latin American politics.

Research experience

  • Graduate Student Research Affiliate, Center for Global Change and Media, UT Austin. Member of the team studying exiled journalism in Latin America, with an emphasis on Central America.
  • Research Assistant, LLILAS Mexico Center, UT Austin. Documented the Center’s institutional history and produced an Impact Report and video for its 45th anniversary.
  • Researcher, Universidad de Costa Rica. Co-coordinated a team analyzing 25 government manifestos and the UN Sustainable Development Goals during the 2022 elections.
  • Researcher, Inter-American Institute of Human Rights. Produced two legal manuals on the right to information, later published as books.
